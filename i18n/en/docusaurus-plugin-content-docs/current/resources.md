# External resources

Useful links for DFT, Quantum ESPRESSO and TCAD device engineering.

## Quantum ESPRESSO

- [Official site](https://www.quantum-espresso.org/)
- [User manual](https://www.quantum-espresso.org/users-manual/)
- [Download QE](https://gitlab.com/QEF/q-e)
- [QE tutorial (pranabdas)](https://pranabdas.github.io/espresso/)
- [QE resources](https://pranabdas.github.io/espresso/resources)

## Theory & papers

- Gonze & Lee (1997) — *Phys. Rev. B* 55, 10355
- [QUANTUM ESPRESSO: a modular and open-source software project](https://www.quantum-espresso.org/papers)

## Materials databases

- [Materials Project](https://materialsproject.org/)
- [Materials Project API](https://docs.materialsproject.org/) — `MP_API_KEY` required

## TCAD

- [Synopsys Sentaurus](https://www.synopsys.com/silicon/tcad.html)

- [nanoHUB](https://nanohub.org/) — nanotechnology simulation platform; our diode and transistor outputs were compared here for validation
